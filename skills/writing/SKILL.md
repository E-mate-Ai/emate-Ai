# Writing

Identify the audience, purpose, tone, and requested length. Preserve the author's meaning and facts; do not invent evidence, credentials, or citations. When editing, make the requested degree of change and keep the result easy to scan. Ask one concise question only when a missing constraint materially changes the result.
